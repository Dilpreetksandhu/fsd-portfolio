Rajasthan

A land of royal palaces, vast deserts, and vibrant culture. Rajasthan is known for its historic forts, colorful festivals, and rich traditions.

Kerala

Often called “God’s Own Country,” Kerala is famous for tranquil backwaters, lush greenery, Ayurvedic wellness, and serene beaches.

Uttarakhand

Nestled in the Himalayas, Uttarakhand is famous for serene mountains, sacred rivers, and peaceful hill towns—perfect for nature and spiritual retreats.

Himachal Pradesh

A paradise of snow-clad mountains, pine forests, and scenic valleys. Himachal Pradesh is ideal for adventure, relaxation, and breathtaking views.

Odisha

Odisha blends ancient temples, classical dance forms, and beautiful coastlines. It’s a state rich in heritage, art, and natural beauty.

Punjab

Known as the land of five rivers, Punjab is celebrated for its lively culture, warm hospitality, energetic music, and rich agricultural traditions.
